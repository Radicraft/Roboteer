# Owner decisions, 2026-09-24

Every agent reads this before building. These override the teardowns where they differ.

## Look
- Main menu: M2 face-off (your machine warm light left, next opponent cold light right). M1 showroom turntable when there is no opponent.
- Garage: G1 assembly bay with G2's part pull-out on focus.
- Build order: prove one machine (starter wedge + flipper) at target in the engine before rebuilding screens.
- No coloured edge strokes or accent lines on buttons, cards or panels. No em dashes in user-facing text.

## Arenas
- Six distinct venues from one shared kit.
- Crowd: rendered person cards near the walls, silhouettes and lights further back.
- 3 to 4 s skippable entrance with pyro before EVERY fight.
- One hazard warning language across all arenas, each arena with its own props and sounds.
- Fight camera: 10 to 15 % CLOSER by default (machines are about a tenth of frame height today). This supersedes "keep the camera"; pitch stays 50.

## Combat
- Hit-stop: Ratchet-strong. Every hit, scaled by tier, up to 120 ms; slow motion when a part comes off. Held on the physics tick clock, replay-safe.
- Aim assist: light steering pull toward the opponent on touch; none on pad unless it tests badly.
- Spinners: a part takes two or three clean hits (not one).
- Axe wind-up: visual only, no balance change.
- Audio: free sources only (Sonniss GDC bundles, Freesound CC0, record licences). Stings plus the text ticker, no voiced announcer yet.
- AI recovery windows: early tiers only (Scrapyard to National).
- Finish: fixed ~3.5 s beat, tap to skip.

## Progression and front end
1. Broke with an unfit machine: free emergency patch-up to 25 %.
2. Workshop repair: charge the career bill.
3. Medals: yes (win, over 70 % health, knockout or flawless). Full pay first time, half after.
4. Prices: armour as a full coat (Steel 184, Reinforced 208, HDPE 61), utilities at factor 20 (60 to 180).
5. First purchase: take the Saw out of the starting loadout.
6. Vortex spinner, Overcharge core, Apex: bring them forward into the campaign.
7. Part mastery Mk II / Mk III: not for 1.0.
8. After the campaign: both Championship Defence and the daily seeded exhibition with a leaderboard.
9. First career match: 2-minute opener.
10. Menu audio: a theme, with a low crowd bed under the stage.
11. Graphics: four modes in Settings (Auto, Quality, Performance, Battery); Battery may run menus at 30 fps.
12. One unified garage.
13. Free players get one event and one ladder fight as a taster.
