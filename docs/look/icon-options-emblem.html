App icon: crossed arsenal emblems

Mockups only; the app's icon is unchanged. The owner asked for emblems built from the game's weapons and parts, crossed like an old poster emblem. Each one crosses two or three pieces of the arsenal (saws, axes, spanners, a drill lance, flipper arms, lifter forks, shock prods, bar spinners, a vertical disc, cogs and a wheel), drawn as simple technical shapes. The styles vary: blueprint line, bold flat, a stamped badge, a one-colour engraving and two-colour poster flatness. The palettes include the owner's blueprint blue, red and black, and steel with hazard amber.

What was borrowed and what was not. Only the crossed composition and the flat emblem feel come from the old poster emblems. None of these uses a hammer and sickle, a five-pointed star, a wheat wreath or any real state, political or military emblem. Those are banned or restricted in several countries and would be an App Store risk. The crossed tools here are the game's own weapons and workshop parts, drawn by hand.

Ten emblems were drawn and one was dropped because it did not read at 60 px (shown at the bottom). The lockups put the emblem in a roundel above the round drawn ROBOTEER lettering. A stencil cut of the name, made for emblems, is shown once under emblem 7.

All nine on a home screen, at 180 px and at real size (60 px)
1

Crossed saws

Blueprint lineStrong pick

Two circular saws on their arms, crossed like swords and drawn as a blueprint, bolted together where they cross.

Trade-off: the blueprint look the owner liked with a much stronger shape; two saw discs side by side can read as a pair of eyes at 60 px.

2

Cog and flippers

Bold flat

Two flipper arms crossed through a big cog: the workshop and the weapon in one badge, steel with hazard amber.

Trade-off: bold and very readable, but a cog with crossed tools is a common engineering badge, so it needs the flipper shapes to feel ours.

3

Saw, axe and spanner

Bold flat

A big saw blade behind a crossed axe and spanner, cut flat in black on red like a fight poster.

Trade-off: the most dramatic, but red and black crossed tools is the closest to the political posters we are avoiding, so it needs care.

4

Drill and spanner

One-colour engraving

A drill lance and a spanner crossed, engraved in one ink with fine hatching, like a plate from an old tool catalogue.

Trade-off: crafted and unusual, but the hatching goes grey at 60 px and a light icon sits apart from the game's dark look.

5

Sprocket crown

Stamped badge

A stamped steel medal: two sets of lifter forks crossed below a small sprocket that sits on top like a crown.

Trade-off: the most 'official' feel, a trophy you would win, but the rim takes room and the forks are small inside it.

7

Spanners over a wheel

Bold flat

Two spanners crossed over a knobbly wheel, red and white on black: a pit crew badge for a team that fights.

Trade-off: instantly readable and warm, but crossed spanners are the classic garage badge, and the weapon side of the game is missing.

Alternative lettering for emblems: a stencil cut, shown once
8

Prods and spinner

Bold flat

Two shock prods crossed behind a bar spinner, in black on hazard amber like a warning poster.

Trade-off: the loudest on a home screen, but hazard amber and black reads as 'danger sign' first and 'game' second.

9

Axes and disc

Blueprint line

Two axes crossed behind a vertical spinning disc with its two teeth, in the owner's blueprint blue and white line.

Trade-off: a strong heraldic read, but crossed axes are a well-worn motif and the disc could be taken for a shield boss.

10

Crossed bars

Bold flatStrong pick

Two bar spinners crossed into an X with a cog at the hub, solid white on blueprint blue: the simplest mark here.

Trade-off: reads at any size and is easy to own, but the X can look like a close button or a propeller without the name.

Drawn and dropped at 60 px

Two strongest: 1 Crossed saws and 10 Crossed bars

  • 1 Crossed saws is the owner's blueprint look with a far stronger shape: two saws on their arms crossed like swords, which says "robot combat" rather than "garage". It reads at 60 px as a pair of blades over an X.
  • 10 Crossed bars is the simplest and most ownable: two bar spinners make the X and a cog makes the hub, solid white on blueprint blue. It is the one that still works as a favicon or an embroidered patch.
  • Also strong: 7 Spanners over a wheel for warmth, and 8 Prods and spinner if loud matters most.
