Pass 5, 27 September: the Workshop test arena

The arena you drive in from the builder (arena/test_arena.tscn: the Yard's floor and walls, no hazard, the target pad on the north wall) had no venue at all: a floor in a black void, the dead spike bezel still in the middle. It is now its own place, a private test bay after hours, dressed with the same shared kit as the six venues (tiers, reflection probe, beam haze, grade keys). Layout, target pad, bodies and gameplay are untouched. Each row is before | after | the Yard (approved) at the same camera, for the family look.

What was built

  • A test floor painted on sealed grey concrete (one new map, workshop_floor.png): a cross at every metre, measuring tapes with 10 cm ticks and numbers along the far south and east edges, pivot rings with 15 degree ticks where the spike patch was, start boxes A and B round both spawns, a yellow keep-out line, and in front of the target pad a hatched strike zone with range lines at 1, 2 and 3 m. Light tyre marks from testing, a few drips. The dead spike bezel is hidden (render only, the tree the physics sees is unchanged).
  • The building: painted block walls with a dark dado, dark cladding above, columns every 5 m, roof trusses, a closed roller door in the east wall.
  • Light: nine LED high-bays on drop rods over the pit (two are real downlights with beam haze on high), more round the room with painted pools under them; two halogen twin-head work lights on tripods just outside the wall, aimed in (real lights on high). Neutral LED key, warm halogen fill, warm grey ambient, a little dust. No crowd, no screen, no pyro.
  • Far side (in the top of the fight frame): a pegboard tool wall with shadow-board outlines (workshop_wall.png), the TEST BAY sign, four benches with vices, parts and drawer units under battens, red roll cabinets, crates on pallets, a tyre stack and gas bottles.
  • The pit crew bay (east): a rubber mat, a machine up on a stand under a boom lamp, a charging cart, a noticeboard with test sheets, a parts rack, two mechanics (MakeHuman cards, no phones; one on medium, none on low).
  • Behind the camera: a low backstop behind the target pad, the pit office with lit windows, a welding bay with its orange curtain, shelving, road cases.
  • Arena walls: the shared riveted panel sleeve and the worn hazard trim on the wall tops, like the family.

What is still short

  1. The dressing is merged primitive blocks (benches, cabinets, crates, the machine on the stand): it reads as a workshop at the fight and wide cameras, plain up close.
  2. The two mechanics are crowd cards with the crowd's idle and cheer poses, not working poses.
  3. The room outside the pit is darker than a real lit workshop, to match the family's night grade; the near side (behind the camera) is mostly dark.
  4. The workshop is not in the warm-up list (render/warmup.gd, the loading screen lead's file): most of its pipelines are shared with the Yard, but check the first frames on the phone.
  5. Nothing measured on the phone.
