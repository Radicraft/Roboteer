Blueprint menus

The owner picked emblem 9 version C, white single weight line on blueprint blue graph paper, and asked for the menus to follow the blueprint theme "to an extent". This page shows how far that could go: three directions, each applied to the same five screens (main menu, builder with a part list, shop, Series board, match result), with interaction states and a light echo in the fight HUD.

Every backdrop is a real render of the live 3D stage, captured today from a Godot window (qa/stage_preview.tscn with the proving slice Tinribs; the fight is the furnace capture from the type review). Everything on top is live HTML and SVG at 956 x 440 pt, the iPhone 18 Pro Max in landscape. The part line drawings are drawn from the real dimensions in Shared/parts.json (Wedge 900 x 600 mm, 180 mm hull, 140 mm wheels). Names, masses, prices and Levels are the game's own. Type is the chosen 01 Blueprint system: Saira Stencil One, Saira Semi Condensed, B612 Mono, all set from three variables at the top of the stylesheet.

House rules kept in all three: no stroked edges on buttons or panels (plates are solid fills; lines appear only in drawings, leaders, dimensions and table rules), nothing skewed, no fading rules, no em dashes on screen.

A

Blueprint overlay

CharacterThe live 3D stage stays the hero; every panel becomes a blueprint plate in the icon's own blue, and leader lines tie the numbers to the real machine.
Trade-offClosest to the icon and the cheapest to build (restyle the shared plate, button and row components, then add a callout layer), but the bright blue plates over a dark stage can read as clean rather than menacing.

Full drafting table

Interaction states

Recommendation

C, Workshop at night. It is the only one of the three that is both blueprint and sinister: the sheets carry the icon's graph paper and white line, but in ink rather than bright blue, so the live 3D stage (the PS4-bar asset the menus should sell) stays the brightest thing on screen. The single hazard red gives selection a meaning it lacks today, and stamps give wins, grudges and scrutineering a physical mark.

Take two things from the others. From A, the leader lines that tie numbers to the real machine (C already uses them). From B, the plan-view damage map for the match report only: it is the one screen where a drawing says more than the 3D.

Rules to hold when it is built: red means selected or in danger and nothing else; stamps only on real events (fit to fight, won, grudge); pins and the slight tilt only on secondary sheets, never on the plate with the primary button.

A is the safe choice if C reads too dark on the device; the two share every layout on this page, so the switch is a token change, not a rebuild.
