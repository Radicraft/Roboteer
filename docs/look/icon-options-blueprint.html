App icon: blueprint variants

Mockups only; the app's icon is unchanged. The owner picked Blueprint from the 2D round and asked for more versions made with Recraft. This round explores the idea widely: different machines (the wedge, a spinner), different views (three-quarter, head-on, from above), different drawing styles (dimensioned, sketch, bold) and different blues, including one in blue ink on white.

Recraft drew the machines as vectors. Each drawing was then recoloured to one blue and one line colour, its lines were fattened so they hold at 60 px, and it was re-centred on a clean square. One variant (6) comes from a Recraft style trained on the reference. There is no text in any icon. The ROBOTEER wordmark is the same single-weight lettering drawn for the reference, so it matches every variant. Nine drawings were tested and two failed at 60 px; they are listed at the bottom.

All variants on a home screen, at 180 px and at real size (60 px)
Ref

The reference

The one the owner picked: the wedge in isometric, one weight of white line on blueprint blue, drawn by hand.

Kept here as the reference for this round, not a new option.

1

Dimensioned

Strong pick

The wedge in three-quarter view with heavy strokes and three dimension arrows, like the first sheet of the build manual.

Trade-off: the dimension arrows are what make it a blueprint, and they are the first thing lost at small sizes.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

2

Blue on white

The negative: indigo ink on white paper, a wedge with its ramp down and a box of electronics on top, standing on a ground line.

Trade-off: fresh and stands out among dark icons, but a white icon can look unfinished next to the rest of the game's dark look.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

3

Pencil sketch

A fast chalk sketch on bright blueprint blue, construction lines overshooting the corners, as if drawn on the workshop wall.

Trade-off: the most human and alive, but the scratchy lines shimmer at small sizes and the drawing is Recraft's, harder to keep on-model.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

4

Front elevation

Head-on elevation: two wheels, the raked pods and a centre line, with dimension lines up the sides, calm and symmetrical.

Trade-off: a strong frame, but head-on the machine is abstract and could be read as a sofa or a cart.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

5

Spinner

A different machine from the game: a tall box with a vertical spinning disc on the front, in isometric with dimension arrows.

Trade-off: shows the game is more than one wedge, but the disc and frame need line weight that the drawing only just has.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

6

Bold flip

Strong pick

Drawn with a Recraft style trained on the reference: fat rounded lines, chunky tyres and the flipper thrown open, friendly and loud.

Trade-off: the best at 60 px, but the heavier, rounder line starts to look like a toy rather than a technical drawing.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

7

Plan on a grid

A plan view from above: the hull, the wedge nose and four wheels, centre lines crossing in a target, laid on graph paper.

Trade-off: very clean and very ownable as a symbol, but from above it is abstract and needs the name next to it to say 'robot'.

Drawing: Recraft vector, recoloured, line weight raised and recomposed by hand.

Tested and dropped at 60 px

Two strongest: 1 Dimensioned and 6 Bold flip

  • 1 Dimensioned keeps the idea honest: the wedge still reads at 60 px, and the dimension arrows say "drawing" rather than "picture" as soon as the icon is a little bigger, as it is in the store.
  • 6 Bold flip is the clearest at home-screen size: fat rounded lines, the flipper thrown open and chunky tyres. It is the reference's own style scaled up, and the most fun.
  • A middle path worth asking for: the Dimensioned drawing with the Bold flip line weight.
