HUD plates: upright, uncut, unstroked

The owner's standing rule (design system section 0): no skewed, slanted, cut or stroked accents, they read as "Claude design". The fight feed still had them: the clock and scrap bugs had a cut top-right corner, the name lower thirds and the POWER DOWN / stage sting band had slanted ends, and so did the intro card and the result banner. Every one is now a plain upright blueprint plate, fixed in the shared drawers, with every rectangle the same size as before so nothing moved. Branch wave-e-hud.

What changed

  • Godot, one drawer. Broadcast.draw_plate draws every plate: the blueprint blue (PLATE, rgba 14, 58, 140 at 82 %, the menus' HUD echo), its graph paper and the soft top light. Fills only, no line. panel_points is a plain rectangle; CUT, SKEW_DEG and skew() are deleted so nothing can cut or lean a plate again. The panel, lower third, score bug and pill helpers all go through it, so hud.gd, intro_overlay.gd and tape_card.gd changed only their fills.
  • States in fill and type, never a line. The arena's moments (POWER DOWN, ENDGAME, the stage stings, a hazard's intro card) sit on the blueprint danger plate (#B8352A) in white. A payoff (the player's moment) sits on the blue plate in scrap amber. Danger type on the blue (the urgent clock, the knockout count) is the plate's own danger red #FF7A66, 4.1:1 on the blue; plain #E5483C would be 2.2:1.
  • Graph paper off the edges. The grid used to start on the plate's top and left edges, which put a bright line along two sides like a frame. It now starts one step in, in Godot and in Swift (BlueprintGrid).
  • Venue big screen. The 3 px gold rule over the name strip is gone; the strip is the blueprint plate with the top light.
  • Swift. CutRect and LowerThirdShape were already drawing plain rectangles behind two switches; both shapes, both switches and Stage.skew are deleted and every former call site uses PlateRect(). The builder's name field has no plate at all (it is type over the stage); its thickness chips and livery rows were the CutRects there.

Kept on purpose (functional, not decoration)

  • Condition bars, the spin-up ring and gas pips on the weapon button, the boost charge ring: gauges that fill.
  • Ink outlines on type and marks that sit over the live arena (the YOU tag, the off-screen arrow, the countdown, the result title and winner line, the knockout count and prompt, the pad hints) and the black outline on the big screen's sting word: legibility over a moving picture, not a frame on a plate.
  • The coach leader lines (they point at a control), the workshop test overlay's diagram strokes, the blueprint leader lines, dimension ticks and table rules in the menus (diagram content).
  • The hatch on locked things (BlueprintHatch): a state texture, not an edge or an accent.
  • The ticker strip (ink at 90 %) and the touch discs (glass): a strip and round thumb furniture, not plates.
