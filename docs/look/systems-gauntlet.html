Systems gauntlet

Roboteer QA, 2026-09-25. Drives, arenas, the house bot, bosses and the screen flow, tested as real fights instead of unit tests. Numbers, repro commands and commit hashes are in docs/qa/systems-gauntlet.md. Frames were captured windowed on 2026-09-25 at eccc0c4; the hull, weapons and AI were being changed by other agents during the run.

Top bugs
  1. The srimech rolls a flipped wedge, box or shell a full turn and lands it upside down again, for ever (D1).
  2. Three of five house bots never leave their corner at the endgame; the house bot hurt someone once in 17 fights (A1).
  3. Omni cannot strafe (D2).
  4. Hover machines are killed by their own landings (D5).
  5. Two machines can sit locked together for up to 95 s with nothing ending it (A2).
  6. Walker turns at a third of its rated rate and stops at a 4 cm lip; 2WD is sluggish and cannot climb (D3, D4).
  7. Cyan edge strokes on every button, which the owner ruled out.
