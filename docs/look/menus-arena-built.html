Menus in the arena

Direction A, the blueprint overlay, built into the app over staging 2: the machine on its plinth on the Coliseum floor, the crowd in the bowl, the truss ring's shafts, the gate lit, pyro on arrival and on a win. Built images are the app's own SwiftUI layer on the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro Max and iPhone 17 Pro) standing over a real desktop Godot render of the same screen, with the leader lines landed from that render's own stage_anchors; the simulator cannot draw the live stage itself.

Fixed from the mockup: the machine is framed large on the main menu; the venue's red and blue light never reaches the machine (its lights leave the machine's render layer out and the ambient is neutral), so the stage key is the brightest light on it and the orange stays orange; the crowd is staged for depth (front rows lit by the floods and falling off, a faint cold rim, phone glints, the back of the bowl sinking to dark) through the venue kit's own crowd interface (now crowd C, 3D people by the rail); the pyro is a narrow fast jet off the gate's own launchers, white CO2 when a screen opens and flame only on a win, never looping; the close screens get moving heads through soft haze cones; a blueprint of the machine is projected on the floor round the plinth.

Leader lines land on the part their label names, by part id, and never cross each other or run through another tag; every capture below is checked by menus-arena/check_leaders.py against the anchors it was fed (all ten main captures pass).
