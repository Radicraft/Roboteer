# Graphics bar: PS4 class (owner, 2026-09-13)

Owner: "overall we need to make sure the graphics are at least ps4 quality". This raises brief §13
from "stylised low-poly, tabletop miniature" to console-quality rendering of the same design language:
chunky readable silhouettes stay, but every surface, light and effect must hold up on a 6.9 inch OLED at
full resolution. The arena, the bots, the stage and the effects all follow this document.

## Rendering pipeline (Godot 4.6, libgodot on iOS, Metal)

- **Device: Forward+** (`rendering/renderer/rendering_method = "forward_plus"`), Mobile kept as the
  fallback (`rendering_method.mobile = "mobile"`) and selected at start by a `GraphicsQuality` autoload
  based on the device model (iPhone with A15 or newer gets Forward+; the simulator gets GL Compatibility).
  Forward+ unlocks volumetric fog (real dry ice), SSAO, SSR on the arena floor and the stage, SDF shadows,
  physical light units and glow with proper HDR.
- Environment: ACES tonemapping, exposure per scene, glow on (threshold so only sparks, bolts, beams and
  the core glow bloom), SSAO on, SSR on the stage floor, ambient from a dark studio sky, colour correction
  LUT optional. Shadows: directional key with 2 to 3 cascades in the arena; spot shadows on the stage.
- Reflection probe over the arena and one over the stage.
- Target 60 fps on iPhone 17; measure on device; drop SSR then volumetrics then shadow cascades first.

## Models: Blender-authored modular parts (replaces procedural visual meshes)

- Blender 4.x installed at `~/Applications/Blender.app`; parts are generated by Python scripts in
  `Tools/blender/` so they are reproducible and tweakable, exported as glTF binary to
  `Arena/assets/parts/<category>/<id>.glb` (category = chassis, drive, weapon, utility, armour, boss, arena).
- Units metres, +X forward, +Y up, +Z right (the Bot convention). Origin at the chassis centre on the
  axle plane. Real bevels (2 to 4 mm), panel gaps, bolt heads at panel corners, weld seams, hydraulic rams,
  wheel hubs with tread. Poly budget: chassis 4 to 8 k tris, wheel 600, weapon 2 to 6 k, panel 300 to 800.
- **Sockets** are empties named `socket_weapon_front`, `socket_weapon_top`, `socket_weapon_shell`,
  `socket_wheel_fl|fr|ml|mr|rl|rr` (per drive slot), `socket_armour_front|sides_l|sides_r|rear|top`,
  `socket_utility_0|1`, `socket_srimech`, matching `Shared/parts.json` wheel_slots. Godot reads them from
  the glTF node tree; the assembler uses them when present and falls back to the catalogue numbers.
- Material slots named by role so Godot swaps materials at runtime: `livery`, `steel`, `titanium`,
  `hdpe`, `polycarbonate`, `rubber`, `dark_metal`, `core_glow`. Textures: one 2048 atlas per category.
- Collision stays the simple convex shapes from the physics code; visual meshes never drive physics.

## Materials and textures

- PBR sets (albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO) generated with Recraft (seamless material photos)
  plus PIL for normal-from-height and roughness, at 1024 or 2048, in `Arena/assets/textures/`:
  brushed steel, scratched titanium, moulded HDPE (colourable), polycarbonate (translucent), tyre rubber,
  arena steel plate with wear, chevron paint, concrete, scorch.
- Damage carries the visual load (brief §13): scratch and scorch decals (Decal nodes) accumulate at damage
  event positions; bent panels via a small vertex displacement on detached plates; dust and sparks with
  trails; bolts emissive gold with glow.
